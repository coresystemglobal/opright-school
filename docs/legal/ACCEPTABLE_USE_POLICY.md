> [Terms of Service](./TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md). It applies to **all** Users — admins,
> principals, teachers, staff, parents/guardians, and students.

By using the SchoolOS platform (the "**Platform**") you agree to these rules. Schools
are responsible for ensuring their Users comply.

---

## 1. General principle

Use the Platform only for legitimate school-management purposes, lawfully, and with
respect for the privacy and safety of others — especially **students, who are
minors**.

---

## 2. You must NOT

### Security & integrity
- Attempt to access data, accounts, tenants, or functions you are not authorised to use.
- Circumvent authentication, role permissions, tenant isolation, or rate limits.
- Probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Platform without our prior written consent.
- Introduce malware, or interfere with or disrupt the Platform's infrastructure.
- Reverse-engineer, scrape at scale, or copy the Platform except as the law allows.

### Data & privacy
- Misuse personal data accessed through the Platform, or use it for any purpose other
  than your legitimate role.
- Share student or staff personal data with unauthorised persons.
- Use student data for marketing, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the School.
- Upload personal data you have no lawful basis or consent to process.

### Content & conduct
- Upload or share content that is unlawful, defamatory, harassing, hateful, obscene, or
  that exploits, endangers, or is harmful to children.
- Impersonate any person or misrepresent your affiliation or role.
- Post false records (e.g. falsified grades, attendance, payments, or identities).
- Infringe intellectual-property or other rights.

### Payments & fees
- Use the fee-collection features to facilitate fraud, money laundering, or any
  unlawful payment.
- Attempt to alter settlement bank details to divert funds, or bypass the
  bank-change approval process.
- Initiate fraudulent chargebacks or misrepresent payment disputes.

### Service abuse
- Use the Platform to send spam or unsolicited bulk messages.
- Resell, sublicense, or provide the Platform to third parties without authorisation.
- Place unreasonable load on the Platform or use it to mine data about other tenants.

---

## 3. Role-specific expectations

- **Admins & Principals** — manage roles and access responsibly; grant least-privilege
  access; promptly remove access for people who leave the School.
- **Teachers & Staff** — access only the students and records relevant to your duties;
  keep records accurate.
- **Parents/Guardians** — access only your own children's information; keep your
  credentials private.
- **Students** — use the Platform for your own learning; do not attempt to access other
  students' or staff data.
