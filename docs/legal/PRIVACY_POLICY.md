## 1. Who is responsible for your data (controller vs processor)

SchoolOS plays **two different roles**:

| Situation | SchoolOS's role |
|---|---|
| Data a **School** uploads about its students, parents, staff, grades, attendance, fees, etc. | **Data processor** — the **School is the data controller**. We process this data only on the School's documented instructions, under our [Data Processing Addendum](./DATA_PROCESSING_ADDENDUM.md). |
| Data we collect to run **our business** (e.g. your account login, billing with the School, support requests, Platform security logs) | **Data controller** — this Policy governs it. |

If you are a parent or student and have questions about how *your School* uses your
data, please contact your School first. We will support the School in responding.

## 2. Personal data we collect

Depending on your role, the Platform may process:

**Account & identity**
- Names, email addresses, phone numbers, role, and a hashed password.
- For students: a **student code** (used to log in instead of an email), date of birth, and class/enrolment.

**Academic & school records (controlled by the School)**
- Attendance, grades, assignments, examination results, report cards, e-learning progress, certificates.
- Library, transport, hostel, inventory, event, sports, disciplinary, and **health** records that a School chooses to store.

**Fee & payment data**
- Fee assignments, invoices, payment status, and ledger entries.
- **We do not store full card numbers.** Card and bank-transfer payments are handled by our Payment Processor (**Paystack**). We receive transaction references, amounts, status, and limited metadata.
- A School's **settlement bank details** (bank code, account number, account name) used to create a payment sub-account.

**Technical & usage data**
- IP address, device/browser information, log and audit data, and cookies/local storage (see the [Cookie Policy](./COOKIE_POLICY.md)).

**Communications**
- Notifications, emails, and support correspondence.

### Special-category & children's data
Health records and disciplinary records may be **sensitive**. Student data is
**children's data**. We apply additional safeguards and process such data only as
instructed by the School and as permitted by law.

## 4. Sharing & disclosure

We share data only as needed:

- **With your School** (for processor-role data) — the School controls who in the School can see what, via roles and permissions.
- **Payment Processor (Paystack)** — to process payments and remit funds.
- **Sub-processors** — hosting, database, email/notification, and storage providers that help us run the Platform. A current list is available on request and in the [DPA](./DATA_PROCESSING_ADDENDUM.md).
- **Professional advisers, auditors, and authorities** — where required by law or to protect rights and safety.
- **In a corporate transaction** — e.g. merger or acquisition, subject to this Policy.

We require our processors and sub-processors to protect data under contract.

## 7. Security

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including:

- **Tenant isolation** — each School's data is logically separated (row-level security).
- **Encryption** — passwords are hashed; sensitive fields and data in transit are
  protected; an application encryption key safeguards designated data.
- **Role-based access control** — Users only access functions and data their role allows.
- **Audit logging** of significant actions.
- **Payment-detail protection** — settlement bank changes require an out-of-band
  approval by the platform operator; the Payment Processor's sub-account codes are not
  exposed to School administrators.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal-data breach occurs, we will notify the
relevant School(s) and, where required, the NDPC and affected individuals, without
undue delay.
