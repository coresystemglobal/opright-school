# SchoolOS — Legal & Policy Documents

This directory contains the legal and policy documents that govern use of the
**SchoolOS** platform (the "Platform") — a multi-tenant school management and
fee-collection SaaS.

| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [Terms of Service](./TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md) | The master agreement between SchoolOS and the schools, staff, parents, and students who use the Platform. |
| [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md) | What personal data we collect, why, how we use and protect it, and your rights. Covers minors' (students') data. |
| [Cookie Policy](./COOKIE_POLICY.md) | How we use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies. |
| [Refund & Payments Policy](./REFUND_AND_PAYMENTS_POLICY.md) | How online fee collection works, the platform commission, remittance to schools, and the refund/chargeback process. |
| [Acceptable Use Policy](./ACCEPTABLE_USE_POLICY.md) | The rules every user must follow when using the Platform. |
| [Data Processing Addendum](./DATA_PROCESSING_ADDENDUM.md) | The data-protection contract between SchoolOS (processor) and each school (controller). |

## ⚠️ Important notice — these are templates

> **These documents are drafts/templates prepared to fit the SchoolOS product. They
> are NOT a substitute for legal advice.** Before publishing or relying on them you
> **must** have them reviewed by a qualified lawyer in your operating jurisdiction
> and complete every placeholder marked like `[THIS]`.

You must, at minimum, fill in:

- `[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]` — the legal entity operating SchoolOS
- `[COMPANY REGISTRATION NUMBER]` — e.g. RC number (CAC, Nigeria)
- `[REGISTERED ADDRESS]`
- `[PRIVACY/SUPPORT/LEGAL EMAIL]` addresses
- `[DPO NAME & CONTACT]` — Data Protection Officer
- `[GOVERNING LAW JURISDICTION]` — these drafts assume the **Federal Republic of
  Nigeria** and the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA)** as the default,
  because the Platform transacts in Naira (₦) via Paystack and uses `.ng` domains.
  Adjust if you operate elsewhere.

## Defined terms used across all documents

- **Platform / SchoolOS / "we" / "us"** — the software service operated by `[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]`.
- **School / Tenant** — an educational institution that subscribes to the Platform. Each School is an isolated tenant.
- **User** — any person who accesses the Platform: a School's administrators, principals, teachers, staff, parents/guardians, and students.
- **Student** — a learner enrolled at a School; typically a **minor**.
- **Payment Processor** — **Paystack**, the licensed third-party that processes card and bank-transfer payments.
- **Commission** — the percentage of a collected fee that SchoolOS retains as its service charge.
- **Effective date / Last updated** — 14 June 2026.
