This Policy explains how the SchoolOS platform (the "**Platform**") uses cookies, local
storage, and similar technologies, and how you can control them.

---

## 1. What these technologies are

- **Cookies** — small text files stored by your browser.
- **Local/session storage** — browser storage the Platform uses to keep you signed in
  and to remember preferences. The Platform typically stores your **authentication
  token (JWT)** in browser storage so you stay logged in.
- **Similar technologies** — pixels or SDK identifiers used by some third-party
  services.

---

## 2. Categories we use

| Category | Purpose | Can you disable? |
|---|---|---|
| **Strictly necessary** | Authentication (keeping you logged in), security, tenant/subdomain resolution, load balancing, fraud prevention. | No — the Platform will not work without these. |
| **Functional** | Remembering preferences such as language or display settings. | Yes (some features may degrade). |
| **Performance / analytics** | Aggregated, mostly anonymised usage statistics to help us improve the Platform. | Yes. |

We do **not** use advertising cookies, and we do **not** use student data for
behavioural advertising.
